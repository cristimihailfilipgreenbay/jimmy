# Cellular respiration

Cellular respiration releases energy from glucose and stores it as ATP.

## Glycolysis

Glycolysis happens in the cytoplasm. One glucose molecule is split into two pyruvate molecules, with a net gain of 2 ATP and 2 NADH. It does not need oxygen.

## Krebs cycle

Pyruvate enters the mitochondrion and becomes acetyl-CoA. The Krebs cycle in the mitochondrial matrix produces CO2, NADH, FADH2 and a little ATP.

## Electron transport chain

The electron transport chain sits in the inner mitochondrial membrane. NADH and FADH2 pass electrons along it, and the energy pumps protons across the membrane. ATP synthase lets the protons flow back and makes most of the ATP, about 26 to 28 per glucose. Oxygen is the final electron acceptor and forms water.

## Fermentation

Without oxygen, cells regenerate NAD+ by fermentation. Animal cells make lactic acid; yeast makes ethanol and CO2.
