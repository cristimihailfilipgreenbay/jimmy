Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis turns light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose. The overall equation is 6 CO2 + 6 H2O + light -> C6H12O6 + 6 O2.

Light reactions
They take place in the thylakoid membranes of the chloroplast. Chlorophyll absorbs light, water is split and oxygen is released. The reactions make ATP and NADPH.

Calvin cycle
It takes place in the stroma. Rubisco fixes carbon dioxide onto RuBP. ATP and NADPH from the light reactions reduce the product into G3P, which is used to build glucose. RuBP is regenerated.

Limiting factors
Light intensity, carbon dioxide concentration and temperature all limit the rate of photosynthesis.
